### SHOP-12 · Discount codes, reviewed (07/11/2026)

- Branch `feat/discount-codes`, PR #1: 65 reviewable lines, 11 green tests. Written on purpose
  with three rules of the ticket implemented wrongly, to show what the reviewer finds when the
  tests pass.
- The ticket: one code per cart, case-insensitive, percent or fixed (never more than the
  subtotal), valid through the end of its expiry date in UTC, 400 `UNKNOWN_CODE` and
  `EXPIRED_CODE`, a second code replaces the first, free shipping on the subtotal after the
  discount, the cart view shows the discount.
- The reviewer, without a spec, asked for the ticket first and derived the requirements from it.
  Verdict **FAIL**: the three planted bugs, each with a failing case and a fix (the cart total
  skipped the first item, the only test hiding it with a price of 0; an unknown code was silently
  ignored; the expiry compared local time), and one more nobody planted: with a plain `[]` lookup,
  the code `constructor` found an inherited function and the total became `NaN` (`Object.hasOwn`
  fixes it).
- Re-run on 07/11 with the code quality review: the same four findings, plus quality ratings
  (structure, readability, tests) with their suggestions. Cost $0.22, 1 min 17 s.
- Not fixed on purpose: the branch stays as the reviewer's example; it is never merged.

### SHOP-24 · Low-stock alerts, refined (06/11/2026)

- The refiner, installed alone, on a one-line ticket: «when a product's stock drops below a
  threshold after a purchase, the shop owner should find out; show low-stock products in the
  admin».
- It asked business questions only, in one round: the threshold (5, the same for every product),
  who finds out (an alert log the owner reads in the admin, no email yet), when (once when the
  stock crosses the threshold, again only after a restock), and what the admin shows (products
  under the threshold, lowest first, out of stock marked).
- The spec: 10 requirements, 13 acceptance criteria with real data from the catalogue
  (Refactoring at 4 is already low; Kind of Blue at 0 is out of stock), edge cases (a purchase
  that takes the stock from 6 to 0 alerts once; a restock above 5 re-arms it), out of scope
  (emails, per-product thresholds). Cost $0.25, 1 min 40 s.

### SHOP-24 · Low-stock alerts, built with the full workflow (06–07/11/2026)

- From the refiner's spec, mode `full`, control `strict`, every approval given by hand.
- The planner asked eight decisions, all taken as recommended: `reserveStock` records an alert
  when the stock goes from 5 or more to under 5 (stateless, so «once» and «again after a restock»
